About Me

From Journey to Creativity

Who I Am

I'm Ayush Niroula, a passionate freelance video editor and visual storyteller based in Nepal. With a deep love for cinematic storytelling and travel, I transform raw footage into compelling narratives that move audiences and drive results.

My journey as a creator began with a simple belief: visual storytelling is the future. Every frame, every transition, every cut is intentional—designed to create an emotional connection with your audience.

My Approach

I bring a unique perspective shaped by trekking adventures across Nepal. This experience has taught me that every story has a rhythm and every project deserves a personalized approach. I blend technical expertise with creative vision to deliver reels that stand out.

Whether you're a brand building awareness, a creator growing your audience, or an entrepreneur launching a product—I specialize in creating content that resonates, engages, and converts.
